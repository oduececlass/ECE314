Official course overview

Students will be introduced to concepts of signal amplification along with detailed analysis and design of operational-amplifier circuits. The main emphasis of the course is to introduce students to the basic operation of PN junctions, bipolar-junction transistors (BJTs), and metal-oxide-semiconductor (MOS) transistors and their application in the design of electronic circuits. Detailed large-signal and small-signal models of these devices will be developed. Analysis and design of basic electronic circuit building blocks with diodes, BJTs, and MOSFETs will be studied. Both discrete and integrated-circuit analog design techniques will be covered. The building blocks of an operational amplifier, along with differential amplifiers, will also be covered.
